\begin{abstract}
  The objective of this document is to present and explain the core concepts and
  implementations of our project, that is, Character Network Extraction, using the book series
  "The Foundation" by Isaac Asimov. We will detail the way we decided to use the resources at
  our disposal to achieve it, our way of thinking and the choices we made. We will
  also present the results we obtained and the conclusions we drew from our work.
  Although this project can largely be improved, it also means that the possibilities
  for working on it were numerous, resulting in a work that might not always be coherent and comprehensive.
\end{abstract}
